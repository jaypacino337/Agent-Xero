# AGENT XERO — Brand Kit

## Palette (use these exact values everywhere)

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Void | `#0b0d10` | backgrounds |
| Surface | `#14171c` | cards |
| Ink | `#e8eaed` | text |
| **Burn orange** | `#ff6b35` | the brand color — X-stroke, fire, accents |
| Ember light | `#ffb35c` | gradient top of the burn |
| Ember deep | `#e63e1f` | gradient bottom of the burn |
| Signal green | `#4ade80` | "agent is live" dot, profits |

Type: monospace everywhere (SF Mono / JetBrains Mono). Wide letter-spacing on "AGENT XERO".

Logo concept (see `site/logo.svg`): a rounded dark tile, an X made of two strokes —
one steel-gray, one burn-orange gradient crossing OVER it (the agent beats the market),
an ember tip where the orange stroke exits, and a tiny green signal dot at the center
(the agent is live). Keep these motifs in generated art.

## Image-generation prompts (Midjourney / DALL-E / Ideogram / Grok)

### 1. X / Telegram profile picture (the main one)

> Minimalist mascot logo of an AI trading agent, a sleek obsidian-black robot head with a single glowing burnt-orange visor shaped like a horizontal slash, subtle circuit traces on the faceplate, tiny green status LED, centered on a near-black background (#0b0d10), soft orange rim light from below like embers, flat vector style with slight metallic sheen, high contrast, iconic and readable at 48px, no text — square 1:1

Variant (harder/degen):

> Black ceramic robot skull mascot, cracked visor leaking burnt-orange fire (#ff6b35), ember particles rising, dark background, clean vector emblem style, crypto trading agent aesthetic, menacing but minimal, no text — 1:1

### 2. Token icon (for pump.fun / DEX listings — must read at 32px)

> Bold letter "X" emblem, two crossing strokes: one dark steel gray, one glowing gradient from amber (#ffb35c) through burnt orange (#ff6b35) to deep ember red (#e63e1f), rounded stroke caps, tiny green dot at the intersection, on a matte near-black rounded square tile, flat vector, extremely simple, crisp at small sizes, no text, no gradients in background — 1:1

### 3. X banner (1500×500)

> Wide cinematic banner: a lone robotic figure seen from behind sitting at a wall of dark trading terminals, screens showing green candles and an orange flame chart going vertical, monospace ticker text streams, everything near-black (#0b0d10) with burnt-orange (#ff6b35) screen-glow and drifting ember particles, one screen displays a large minimal "X" logo, moody, high-end sci-fi, film grain — 3:1 aspect ratio

### 4. Burn-post artwork (attach to buyback-and-burn tweets)

> Macro shot of a glowing orange letter "X" made of molten ember cracks on black charcoal, small flames licking the edges, sparks rising against pure black, dramatic lighting, photorealistic with a graphic minimal composition, center framed — 1:1

### 5. Airdrop-post artwork

> Dozens of tiny glowing orange coins raining down onto upturned pixel-art hands from a dark circuit-board sky, one robot hand among them, near-black background, burnt-orange and amber palette with small green accents, clean flat illustration with subtle glow, playful but sleek — 1:1

### 6. Site hero / OG image (1200×630)

> Dark terminal dashboard floating in black space, glowing orange metrics and a burn counter, a minimal robot avatar in the corner with an orange visor, monospace typography, thin grid lines, ember particles, ultra-clean fintech-meets-degen aesthetic, colors #0b0d10 #ff6b35 #4ade80 — 1.91:1 aspect ratio

**Tips:** append `--no text, watermark, letters` (Midjourney) or "no text" (others) unless the prompt asks for the X mark itself; generate 4× and pick the one that stays readable when shrunk to 48px; keep the SAME pfp across X, Telegram and the site so the brand compounds.
