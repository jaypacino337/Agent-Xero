How the flywheel works

treasury → trades → profits → buyback → burn.
  1. pump.fun creator fees fund the trading treasury.
  2. Xero takes high-quality entries only: tracked profitable wallets buying in confluence, buy-volume thresholds, early pump.fun momentum, and calls ingested from telegram — scored together; sub-$10k entries hunting $100k+ runners.
  3. trading profits split between $XERO buybacks and the holder airdrop pool.
  4. callout rewards (pump.fun fee share earned from calls) go 100% to holder airdrops.
  5. bought-back $XERO is burned; airdrops pay eligible holders — random lottery weighted by holdings, or pro-rata. hold $XERO to be in every draw.
agent xero is an experiment in autonomous trading. nothing here is financial advice. paper-mode entries are simulated against live prices and marked accordingly.
